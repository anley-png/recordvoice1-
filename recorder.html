Arabic Letter Island · Recorder

Tips for a clean recording
  • Quiet room, phone about 10–15 cm from the mouth, same distance every time.
  • Letter sounds: say the sound shown in the Amharic and English lines (for example “ba”, “baa”). The row with “Standalone” is the letter name.
  • Words: read the Arabic word at teaching speed. The Amharic line is the reading guide and meaning; the English line is only the meaning, so do not say it.
  • Many rows of one letter have the same sound (initial, medial, final). Record one, then tap Copy to same-sound rows to fill the empty ones.
  • Silence is trimmed and the volume is levelled automatically. Re-record any clip to replace it. Progress is saved on this device.
  • Keys: Space = record/stop, ← → = previous/next, P = play.

Unzip into your repo so the files land in audio/. The zip also contains RELEASE_FORM.txt, a template for the speaker to sign (not legal advice: have it checked).
